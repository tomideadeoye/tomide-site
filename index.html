About

I am a lawyer and an engineer, which means I usually end up working in the seam between the two: regulatory obligations that have to become working code, and software systems that have to survive legal scrutiny.

I read law at Obafemi Awolowo University (LL.B, Second Class Upper), qualified as a barrister at the Nigerian Law School, and was called to the Bar in 2025. Alongside that, I have spent the last few years building software — payments infrastructure, compliance tooling, and lately a law library that treats legal text as versioned data rather than documents.

My current interests are AI governance, the rule of law under automated decision-making, and building institutions that survive contact with real code.
